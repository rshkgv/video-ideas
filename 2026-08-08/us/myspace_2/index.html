Комната становится Y2K

Пользователь стоит перед зеркалом в самой обычной современной спальне. Начиная с одного края кадра, комната позади него buchstäblich "перекрашивается" в эстетику 2000-х — совсем как в реальных роликах тренда, где ленты воспоминаний из старого MySpace-профиля перемешаны с розовым неоновым декором и леопардовым принтом. Розовые стены, гирлянда лампочек, неоновая табличка в виде кролика и леопардовый ковёр наплывают волной слева направо, пока вся комната позади пользователя не превращается в подростковую спальню начала 2000-х, а сам пользователь остаётся собой — только в кадре меняется мир вокруг. Общий повторяющийся элемент реальных роликов тренда — Y2K-эстетика спальни и цветовая гамма "розовый плюс чёрно-белый леопард/зебра" — сохранён, но сценарий полностью новый: превращается не экран компьютера, а вся комната.

Промпт для первого кадра GPT Image 2 
Turn the photo of the person into a knee-up "cowboy shot" portrait, standing in front of a plain, modern bedroom mirror, facing slightly toward the mirror but with their face turned enough toward camera to stay clearly legible. Preserve their exact identity: same face shape, skin tone, eye shape and color, hairstyle and hair texture, jawline, and any distinctive features (freckles, moles, facial hair, glasses) unchanged from the source photo — realistic hands and natural posture. They wear simple, contemporary casual clothes — a plain white t-shirt and jeans — deliberately neutral so the room's later transformation reads as the spectacle, not their outfit. Their expression is relaxed, mildly curious, as if just checking their reflection, mouth closed. The bedroom behind them, for now, is ordinary and modern: plain white walls, a grey bed with plain linens, a simple wooden nightstand with a lamp, beige carpet — nothing Y2K yet, that transformation happens later in the video. Lighting is soft, even, daytime bedroom lighting with gentle shadow falloff. Shallow depth of field keeps the person sharp while the plain room behind softens slightly. Render photoreal, natural skin texture with no over-smoothing, subtle grain, vertical 9:16 framing, neutral, slightly cool color grading appropriate to an ordinary modern bedroom.
Промпт для видео Kling · Grok 
Create a short cinematic video based on the uploaded image.
The scene is an ordinary modern bedroom with a mirror. The person stands knee-up framing, facing slightly toward the mirror, face turned toward camera, wearing plain white t-shirt and jeans, in a plain white-walled room.

Camera and motion structure:
Keep the overall shot static. No zoom, no pan, no camera movement — the room's transformation is the only thing that moves, so stillness lets the contrast read clearly.
1. FRAMES 0–20%: The person stands in the plain modern bedroom, relaxed, glancing at their reflection and then to camera. Nothing in the room has changed yet.
2. FRAMES 20–50%: Starting from the left edge of the frame and creeping steadily rightward across the background, the room begins visibly transforming: the plain white wall turns hot pink, a string of small round fairy lights switches on and drapes into view along the ceiling edge, and a leopard-print rug materializes on the floor — all spreading as a continuous wave moving from left to right, never jumping straight to the finished look.
3. FRAMES 50–80%: The transformation wave continues across the frame — a glowing pink neon sign shaped like a rabbit switches on against the now-pink wall, a scatter of framed collage-style photos and string-light clusters appears on the wall, and the leopard rug's pattern fully resolves underfoot. The person remains standing exactly where they were, watching the change happen around them with growing delighted surprise, still facing toward camera.
4. FRAMES 80–100%: The transformation wave reaches the right edge of the frame, completing the room into a full Y2K bedroom — pink walls, glowing neon rabbit sign, fairy lights, leopard rug all now fully visible. The person's expression settles into a wide, silent, delighted smile as they look around the transformed room and then back to camera. The completed Y2K room remains fully visible and unchanged until the last frame.

Character:
Preserve the person's face, hair, skin tone, and identity exactly from the source image throughout the entire video. Do not alter their appearance, age, clothing, or proportions — only the room around them changes.
The person's only actions are: standing still, a growing delighted expression, and looking between the changing room and the camera. No walking, no touching the walls or objects, no other gestures.

Key visual events (in order):
- The left-to-right transformation wave sweeping across the background, converting the plain wall, floor, and ceiling into pink Y2K bedroom decor — this creeping transformation is the hero moment of the video and must be clearly visible advancing across the frame from one side to the other, never an instant cut between "before" and "after"
- The glowing pink neon rabbit sign switching on partway through the wave
- The leopard-print rug's pattern resolving fully underfoot as the wave passes over the floor
- The person's growing silent, delighted smile as the transformation completes

Style:
Whimsical, dreamy, saturated Y2K nostalgia — warm pink neon glow contrasted against the plain modern room that preceded it. The visual pleasure comes from watching an ordinary space become a specific, recognizable early-2000s bedroom aesthetic in one continuous sweep.

Restrictions:
- Do not change the person's face, hair, skin tone, age, clothing, or identity at any point — only the background room transforms
- Keep the overall shot completely static — no zoom, no pan, no camera movement of any kind
- No speaking, no singing, no lip-sync, no mouthing words — silent delighted reaction only, shown through facial expression, never through mouth shapes forming words
- The person's face and body must remain clearly visible and be the visual focus of the entire video even as the background transforms
- No speech bubbles
- The room's transformation must sweep continuously from the left edge of the frame to the right edge — it must not appear as a sudden jump-cut, a fade, or a flash between two static states; the wave itself must be visible in motion
- The pink neon rabbit sign, once switched on, must remain lit and visible until the last frame — do not let it flicker off or disappear
- No other on-screen text or captions
- No additional gestures, actions, or beats beyond those explicitly described above — no clapping, no spinning, no touching the mirror
- No other people added to the scene
- No smoke, sparkle bursts, or magical particle effects accompanying the transformation — the room changes through the creeping wave alone, not through fantasy visual effects layered on top
