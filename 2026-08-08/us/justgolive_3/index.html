#justgolive

Тихое утро в эфире

Пользователь сидит на полу у окна в мягком раннем утреннем свете, обхватив ладонями кружку кофе, и просто ведёт тихий, немногословный прямой эфир — без трюков и происшествий, только сам момент. Камера почти незаметно, медленно наезжает, а тёплый рассветный луч солнца постепенно расползается по кадру снаружи вовнутрь, пока не ложится прямо на лицо героя. Именно в этот момент счётчик зрителей в углу экрана, который до этого рос совсем незаметно, резко перескакивает вверх и продолжает стабильно тикать — будто именно эта спокойная сцена неожиданно "зацепила" зрителей. Визуальный якорь хэштега — интерфейс прямого эфира TikTok (пульсирующий бейдж LIVE и растущий счётчик зрителей) здесь работает не через комедийный трюк или происшествие, как в двух других идеях, а через тёплый, почти медитативный контраст: тихая сцена и бурно реагирующий эфир.

Промпт для видео Kling · Grok 
Create a short cinematic video based on the uploaded image.
The scene is a quiet bedroom or living room corner at first light. The person sits cross-legged on the floor beside a tall window, chest-up framing, both hands wrapped around a ceramic mug, a small "LIVE" badge and a modest viewer counter already visible in the top-left corner.

Camera and motion structure:
The video is built on one slow, continuous, barely-perceptible push-in — no cuts, no pans, no whip moves.
1. FRAMES 0–25%: Cool, dim pre-dawn blue-grey light fills the room. The person sits calmly, occasionally taking a small sip from the mug, expression relaxed and unhurried. The "LIVE" badge pulses gently; the viewer counter reads a low number and only creeps up by a couple of digits at a time. The camera begins its slow push-in, barely noticeable at this stage.
2. FRAMES 25–55%: A warm golden edge of sunrise light begins creeping in from the window side of the frame, spreading gradually across the floor and up the nearby wall — a visibly advancing wave of warm light against the still-cool room. The viewer counter continues its slow, steady climb, ticking a little faster than before. The person remains still, sipping occasionally, unaware of anything except the quiet morning.
3. FRAMES 55–80%: The creeping golden light continues its spread, now reaching the person's shoulder and beginning to climb toward their jaw and cheek. The camera's push-in is now clearly perceptible, tightening toward the face. The viewer counter keeps climbing steadily.
4. FRAMES 80–100%: The golden light fully reaches and settles across the person's face — natural, warm, directional morning light, not a blown-out flare — exactly as the viewer counter makes one clear, sudden upward jump to a much higher number and small heart icons begin gently floating up from the bottom of the frame at a slow, soft pace (not a dense burst). The person's expression shifts into a soft, warm, closed-mouth smile; their eyes close briefly in quiet contentment as the light hits their face, then reopen toward the camera before the final frame. The "LIVE" badge, viewer counter, and floating hearts all remain visible until the last frame.

Character:
Preserve the person's face, hair, skin tone, and identity exactly from the source image throughout the entire video. Do not alter their appearance, age, or proportions. Hands must remain anatomically correct, holding the mug naturally throughout.
The person's only actions are: sitting still, occasionally sipping from the mug, and the one brief eyes-closing-then-reopening reaction as the light reaches their face. No standing, no walking, no other gestures.

Key visual events (in order):
- The warm golden sunrise light visibly creeping across the room from the window, spreading over the floor, wall, shoulder, and finally the face — this creeping light is the hero moment of the video and must be clearly and continuously visible advancing across the frame, not an instant lighting change
- The viewer counter's steady, unhurried climb through most of the video, followed by one clear upward jump timed to the light reaching the person's face
- The person's quiet, closed-mouth smile and brief peaceful eyes-closing-then-reopening as the light lands on their face

Style:
Tender, quiet, almost meditative morning atmosphere — natural window light, soft grain, no broadcast gloss, no comedic beats. The contrast between the stillness of the scene and the livestream UI quietly reacting is the entire point; nothing dramatic happens except light and a number changing.

Restrictions:
- Do not change the person's face, hair, skin tone, age, or identity at any point
- Keep the camera motion limited to the single slow, continuous push-in described above — no pans, no zoom bursts, no handheld shake
- No speaking, no singing, no lip-sync, no mouthing words — the person's only expression is the silent, closed-mouth smile and the brief eyes-closing-then-reopening reaction
- The person's face and the mug in their hands must remain clearly visible and be the visual focus of the entire video
- No speech bubbles
- The brief eyes-closing moment must read as a peaceful, contented blink during a smile — not as the person falling asleep, dozing off, or looking away distracted; the eyes must clearly reopen and look toward the camera before the last frame
- The golden light reaching the person's face must look like natural, warm directional sunlight settling on skin — not a blown-out lens flare, not an overexposed white flash, and not a colored filter effect
- The "LIVE" badge is visible and pulsing from the first frame; the viewer counter is visible throughout and makes its one clear upward jump only in the final 20%, timed to the light reaching the face; the floating hearts appear only in that same final 20%, at a slow, gentle pace — never a dense burst; all three remain visible until the last frame
- No other on-screen text or captions besides the specified "LIVE" badge, viewer counter, and heart icons
- No additional gestures, actions, or beats beyond those explicitly described above — no waving, no peace signs, no extra sips beyond what is described
- No other people, pets, or objects added to the scene
- No dramatic weather effects, no lens flare bursts, no sparkle or glitter effects — only the natural creeping sunlight and the calm livestream UI
