#justgolive

Фокус на камеру

Пользователь стоит дома перед камерой телефона и показывает простой фокус — маленький предмет (монета) исчезает из открытой ладони, пока в углу экрана пульсирует красный значок «LIVE», а счётчик зрителей стремительно растёт вверх с каждой секундой, будто трюк набирает вирусный отклик прямо на глазах. Кадр статичен и фронтален, как в реальных роликах тренда — человек стоит на месте и просто показывает трюк камере. Визуальный якорь — интерфейс прямого эфира TikTok (пульсирующий бейдж LIVE, растущий счётчик зрителей, всплывающие сердечки) как буквальное воплощение хэштега #justgolive.

Промпт для видео Kling · Grok 
Create a short cinematic video based on the uploaded image.
The scene is an ordinary living room. The person stands facing the camera, chest-up framing, one hand raised with a coin resting in the open palm, a small pulsing "LIVE" badge and viewer counter already visible in the top-left corner of frame.

Camera and motion structure:
Keep the overall shot static. No zoom, no pan, no camera movement — the stillness matches the deadpan, direct-to-camera style of the trick.
1. FRAMES 0–20%: The person holds their palm open and flat, coin clearly resting in the center, looking between their hand and the camera with a performative half-smile. The "LIVE" badge pulses gently; the viewer counter reads a low number.
2. FRAMES 20–45%: The person's other hand sweeps once, briefly, over the open palm — a single clean pass, like a magician's gesture — then pulls away.
3. FRAMES 45–65%: The instant the sweeping hand clears the palm, the coin has vanished — the open palm is now empty, shown clearly and unambiguously to the camera. This disappearance happens in that single beat, not gradually.
4. FRAMES 65–100%: The person's expression shifts into a delighted, silent laugh of disbelief at their own trick, shaking their head slightly. At the same time, the viewer counter in the top-left corner begins rapidly ticking upward, and small heart icons start floating up from the bottom of the frame with increasing frequency, as if the clip were suddenly going viral in real time. The "LIVE" badge and the escalating counter/hearts remain visible and active until the last frame.

Character:
Preserve the person's face, hair, skin tone, and identity exactly from the source image throughout the entire video. Do not alter their appearance, age, or proportions. Hands and fingers must remain anatomically correct and naturally proportioned throughout, including during the sweeping gesture.
The person's only actions are: holding the palm open, one clean sweeping hand gesture over it, then a silent delighted reaction. No walking, no other gestures.

Key visual events (in order):
- The coin vanishing from the open palm in a single clean beat right after the sweeping hand gesture — this vanish is the hero moment of the video and must be clearly and unmistakably visible: the palm shown empty, unobstructed, to the camera immediately after
- The person's silent, delighted reaction to their own trick
- The viewer counter rapidly increasing and heart icons floating up with increasing frequency in the final third, as the broadcast UI reacts to the "viral" moment

Style:
Deadpan-comedic, low-key home-video energy shot like an actual TikTok Live capture — plain lighting, ordinary room, no cinematic gloss. The humor comes from the flat, casual delivery of a real magic-style vanish combined with the broadcast UI escalating around it.

Restrictions:
- Do not change the person's face, hair, skin tone, age, or identity at any point
- Keep the overall shot static — no zoom, no pan, no camera movement of any kind
- No speaking, no singing, no lip-sync, no mouthing words — silent emotional reaction only (the delighted laugh must be silent, shown through expression and head movement, not through mouth shapes forming words)
- The person's face and raised hand must remain clearly visible and be the visual focus of the entire video
- No speech bubbles
- The coin's disappearance from the open palm must be clearly and unmistakably visible as a clean vanish — this is the single most important visual element of the shot; do not let the hand-sweep gesture read as simply picking up or pocketing the coin, and do not let the coin remain even partially visible after the sweep
- The "LIVE" badge is visible and pulsing from the first frame; the viewer counter and floating hearts appear/escalate only in the final 35% and must remain visible, still active, until the last frame
- No other on-screen text or captions besides the specified "LIVE" badge, viewer counter, and heart icons
- No additional gestures, actions, or beats beyond those explicitly described above — no thumbs-up, no bowing, no extra flourishes
- No other people, pets, or objects added to the scene
- No sparkle, smoke, or fire effects around the vanish — it must read as a clean sleight-of-hand illusion, not a supernatural or pyrotechnic effect
