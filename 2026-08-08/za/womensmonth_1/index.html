Коробка с сюрпризом

Пользователь сидит на диване с закрытой картонной коробкой на коленях, снятой явно для видео-обзора: рядом лежит телефон на мини-штативе. Он вскрывает скотч, достаёт из коробки предметы один за другим — и на последнем предмете коробка вдруг вспыхивает изнутри мягким розово-золотым светом, а сам предмет в руках пользователя превращается в изящный светящийся значок-медаль с гравировкой в духе «месяца женщин». Пользователь удивлённо, затем тепло улыбается и поднимает медаль к камере крупным планом. Реальный контент хэштега в основном — это распаковки/промо-обзоры, а не буквальное празднование, поэтому первая идея прямо адаптирует этот формат «unboxing», добавляя единственный магический твист-реванш в конце.

Промпт для видео Kling · Grok 
Create a short cinematic video based on the uploaded image. The scene is an ordinary living room; the person sits on a sofa with a closed cardboard box on their lap, about to open it for what reads as a filmed unboxing.

Camera and motion structure:
FRAMES 0–20%: Keep the camera static, medium shot. The person peels back the tape on the box and opens the flaps, glancing inside with mild curiosity.
FRAMES 20–50%: The person reaches in and pulls out one or two ordinary-looking small items (visible only in silhouette/generic shape, nothing brand-specific) and sets them briefly on the sofa beside them, continuing to look through the box with growing interest.
FRAMES 50–75%: The person reaches in one final time and lifts out the last item — as their hand closes around it, a soft pink-and-gold light begins glowing from inside the box, spilling out and wrapping around the object in their hand. The object's shape smoothly reshapes and solidifies into a small, elegant glowing medal/pendant with a simple engraved emblem, still emitting a gentle warm glow.
FRAMES 75–100%: The person's expression shifts from surprise to a warm, genuine smile as they realize what's now in their hand. They lift the glowing medal up toward the camera in a clear, close presentation, holding it steady so its glow and engraved detail are clearly visible, smiling directly at the lens.

Character: Preserve the person's face, hair, skin tone, and identity exactly from the source image throughout the entire video. Movement is naturalistic hand-and-arm action — opening the box, retrieving items, and the final reveal-and-lift gesture. No standing, no large body movement.

Key visual events (in order):
1. The person opening the box and retrieving a couple of ordinary items (frames 20–50%)
2. The final item transforming in a wrap of pink-gold light into a glowing engraved medal while still in the person's hand (frames 50–75%) — this is the hero moment
3. The person lifting the glowing medal clearly into view toward the camera with a warm smile (frames 75–100%)

Style: Bright, casual, authentic "unboxing video" aesthetic for most of the shot, with one warm, glowing magical-realism moment for the transformation. The mood shifts from everyday curiosity to a genuine, warm sense of being honored or celebrated.

Restrictions:
- Do not change the person's face, hair, skin tone, or identity at any point
- No speaking, no singing, no lip-sync, no mouthing words — silent reactions and the final smile only
- The person must remain in frame, recognizable, and the visual focus for the entire video, including during the close-up presentation of the medal — their face must stay visible even while the medal is held up
- No speech bubbles
- The transformation of the final item into the glowing engraved medal is the hero moment and must be clearly and unmistakably visible — the medal must be fully formed, glowing, and held steadily toward camera by the 100% mark, not partially formed or obscured
- Exactly one item transforms — the two earlier retrieved items remain ordinary and do not glow or change; this distinction must be visually clear
- No additional gestures, actions, or beats beyond opening the box, retrieving items, the transformation, and the final presentation lift — no extra props, no other people entering frame
- No aggressive or fast movements when reaching into the box
- No text, logos, or graphics on the box or its contents at any point, other than the medal's own simple engraved emblem
- No brand names, logos, or real company references anywhere in the scene
