El tablero que crece

Пользователь стоит у себя дома с маленьким микрофоном в руке — жест, повторяющий реальные ролики хэштега, где герои держат микрофон и уверенно жестикулируют, представляя что-то зрителю (у церкви, в эфире, в обзоре продукта). Камера медленно наезжает. Пользователь с гордостью указывает рукой на пустую белую доску за спиной — и по мере наезда камеры на доске сам собой начинает проступать светящийся золотой график роста: линия ползёт вверх зигзагом, отметки на доске одна за другой загораются, пока не превращается в жирную стрелку, устремлённую в верхний угол. Общий для хэштега приём — уверенный человек с микрофоном, жестикулирующий перед камерой — сохранён, но сюжет (светящийся график успеха на доске) придуман заново.

Preserve their exact identity: same face shape, skin tone, eye shape and color, natural hairstyle and hair texture, jawline, and any distinctive features (freckles, moles, facial hair) unchanged from the source photo. Add a realistic-hands note: both hands — one holding the microphone, one gesturing — must show correct anatomy and natural proportions.

Wardrobe: a smart-casual outfit — a button-up shirt or a clean sweater, nothing overly formal, appropriate for a confident home "big announcement" moment.

Setting: a bright, simple home interior corner — a plain white or light-grey whiteboard mounted on the wall behind and slightly beside the person, currently blank, a hint of a desk edge or a potted plant at the frame's border, natural daylight from an unseen window.

Lighting: bright, even, natural daylight mixed with warm indoor light, clean and optimistic, minimal shadow.

Camera specs: eye-level, roughly 35mm-equivalent framing, natural depth of field with the blank whiteboard clearly visible but slightly softer than the person's crisp, in-focus face.

Промпт для видео Kling · Grok 
Create a short cinematic video based on the uploaded image. The scene is a bright home interior — the person stands holding a small microphone, gesturing toward a blank whiteboard mounted on the wall beside them.

Camera and motion structure:
The camera performs one slow, continuous, deliberate push-in for the entire video, drawing gradually closer to the person and the whiteboard behind them — no cuts, no other camera moves, just one unbroken creeping approach.
1. FRAMES 0–20%: Medium shot. The person stands confidently, microphone in one hand, the other hand gesturing toward the still-blank whiteboard beside them. The push-in begins, slow and smooth.
2. FRAMES 20–50%: As the camera continues drawing closer, a single thin golden line begins tracing itself across the whiteboard in a zigzagging, ascending path — starting low on the left and creeping steadily upward and to the right, glowing faintly as it draws itself, as though an invisible hand were sketching it in light.
3. FRAMES 50–80%: The glowing line continues its creeping spread further up and across the board, small glowing tick-marks lighting up beneath it one at a time as it passes each one, the golden glow growing steadily brighter and more saturated the further the line travels.
4. FRAMES 80–100%: The line completes its path in the top-right corner of the board, culminating in a bold glowing upward arrowhead. The person's gesture settles into a firm, proud point directly at the arrowhead, their expression confident and satisfied. The push-in slows to a near-stop and holds on this final framing, the glowing chart steady and complete, until the last frame.

Character:
Preserve the person's face, hair, skin tone, and identity exactly from the source image throughout the entire video. Do not alter their appearance at any point.
The person's only movements are: holding the microphone steady, and their gesturing hand moving from an open presenting gesture to a firm point at the finished arrowhead in the final beat. No walking, no other gestures, no change in stance.

Key visual events (in order):
- The whiteboard staying completely blank for the first 20% of the video
- The golden growth line creeping and glowing its way across the board in one continuous, unbroken path from frames 20–80% — this steady, glowing creep spreading across the board is the hero moment of the video and must be clearly, unmistakably visible as a gradual spread, not an instant full-chart pop-in
- The line completing in a bold glowing upward arrow exactly once, in the top-right corner, around the 80% mark
- The person's hand settling into a firm point at the completed arrow in the final beat

Style:
Bright, confident, motivational-content aesthetic — clean daylight interior, a single glowing golden accent color against an otherwise neutral, everyday room. Assured and energetic, not flashy or cluttered.

Restrictions:
- Do not change the person's face, hair, skin tone, or identity at any point
- No speaking, no singing, no lip-sync, no mouthing words — the person's mouth stays closed or naturally relaxed throughout; any implied message is conveyed through gesture and expression only, never through visible speech
- The camera performs exactly one slow, continuous push-in for the whole video — no cuts, no zoom-outs, no pans, no additional camera moves
- The person must remain in frame, clearly visible, and the visual focus for the entire video, even as the camera moves closer
- No speech bubbles
- The glowing growth line must appear gradually, creeping and spreading across the whiteboard between frames 20–80% — it must never appear instantly or all at once, and once complete it must remain visible and unchanged until the last frame

- No additional gestures, actions, or beats beyond those explicitly described above
- No other people appear in frame
- No real brand names, logos, company names, or currency symbols of any specific country appear on the whiteboard — the chart stays abstract and generic
