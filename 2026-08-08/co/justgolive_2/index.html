La plaza sigue igual, el teléfono no

Общая нить реальных видео тега — люди оживлённо жестикулируют руками, разговаривая с кем-то на камеру или рядом, посреди самых обычных мест. Здесь пользователь стоит на людной дневной колумбийской площади с разноцветными фасадами и уличными торговцами, оживлённо жестикулируя двумя руками в разговоре, телефон стоит прислонённый к сумке рядом. Камера медленно наезжает, площадь вокруг продолжает жить своей обычной жизнью — прохожие, торговцы, — но экран телефона рядом с пользователем сам по себе преображается: на нём вспыхивает красный LIVE-значок и начинает расти счётчик зрителей, будто именно этот телефон единственный «заметил», что трансляция началась. Якорь — оживлённая дневная площадь с разноцветными фасадами, характерная для повторяющихся уличных сцен тега, плюс контраст между неизменной площадью и преображающимся экраном телефона.

Create a short cinematic video based on the uploaded image. The scene is a busy daytime plaza in Colombia: the person stands gesturing animatedly in conversation, a phone propped against their bag beside them, while the plaza carries on exactly as before and only the phone's screen quietly transforms.

Camera and motion structure:
The camera performs one slow, continuous push-in across the entire video, gradually tightening from a waist-up shot toward a closer framing on the person and the propped phone beside them — smooth and deliberate, never rushed.
1. FRAMES 0–25%: The person gestures with both hands mid-conversation, plaza life continuing normally behind them — pedestrians crossing, the vendor's cart, umbrellas swaying slightly. The phone screen beside them stays dark and unremarkable. The camera begins its slow push-in.
2. FRAMES 25–55%: The person continues gesturing, now with one hand emphasizing a point near their chest. The plaza behind remains completely unchanged — same pedestrians pace, same light. The phone screen beside them flickers once and a small red dot appears in its corner.
3. FRAMES 55–80%: The person lowers their hands and glances briefly toward the phone, noticing it, then looks back up with a growing smile. The plaza is still entirely unchanged in the background. The phone's screen now clearly shows a red pulsing "LIVE" badge and a small viewer-count number that has begun ticking upward.
4. FRAMES 80–100%: The camera's push-in settles on a closer framing of the person and the phone together. The person smiles at the camera, unbothered by the still-unchanged plaza around them. The LIVE badge and viewer count are now fully visible and legible, the count steadily climbing, and remain locked on screen until the last frame.

Character:
Preserve the person's face, hair, skin tone, and identity exactly from the source image throughout the entire video. Do not alter their appearance. Their only movements are: gesturing with both hands, gesturing with one hand, glancing at the phone, and smiling at the camera — performed exactly once each, in that order, with no additional gestures.

Key visual events (in order):

Style:
Bright, warm, documentary-style street realism for the plaza and the person, contrasted with a small pocket of glossy, exaggerated TikTok-LIVE UI graphics confined entirely to the phone screen. The whimsical joke is that nothing in the real world changes — only the phone knows something has started.
