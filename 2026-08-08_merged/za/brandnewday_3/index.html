#brandnewday

Комната становится страницей

Человек стоит у себя в комнате — и с краёв кадра внутрь начинают вползать чёрные типографские рамки комикса, деля пространство на панели, покрывая стены крупным растровым узором точек, будто вся комната стала страницей графического романа. На стене висел плакат с Человеком-пауком — теперь он отслаивается от бумаги в том же чернильном, растровом стиле и буквально сходит со стены в комнату, оставаясь плоским, рисованным, тогда как сам человек остаётся полностью живым и объёмным на его фоне. Нарисованный герой протягивает кулак для комиксового удара — человек отвечает своим, живым кулаком — контакт застывает на долю секунды вспышкой белой звезды без единой буквы. Камера отступает, показывая всю комнату целиком уже как один большой панельный разворот с чёрной рамкой по краям кадра. Связь с трендом: название фильма буквально означает «начало нового дня» для героя — здесь эта идея нового начала показана как перерисовка целой комнаты в графический роман, а сам плакат с героем оживает не как реальный человек, а как рисунок, шагнувший со страницы.

Промпт для первого кадра GPT Image 2 
Turn the photo of the person into a portrait of them standing in an ordinary bedroom, facing camera, vertical 9:16 composition, chest-up framing, centred.

Pose & composition: standing relaxed but present, weight even, one arm slightly bent as if about to gesture, gaze directly at the lens.

Expression: calm, alert curiosity — the look of someone who senses something in the room is about to change.

Attire: plain everyday clothes, nothing branded.

Setting & background: an ordinary bedroom with plain walls; on the wall behind and to the side hangs a Spider-Man movie poster in the film's own red-and-blue palette, fully intact and flat against the wall, not yet reacting to anything.

Style note: the person themselves must render as fully photorealistic, real skin and real fabric. The poster on the wall is an ordinary printed poster at this stage — flat, glossy, unremarkable.

Lighting: soft, neutral indoor daylight through a window, even and calm.

Camera & lens: 50mm-equivalent, natural depth of field, the poster legible but secondary to the person.

Identity preservation: keep the person's identity exactly as in the source photo — same age, face shape and proportions, skin tone and undertone, eye shape and eye colour, eyebrow shape, nose and jawline, and the exact hairstyle including its colour, parting, length and texture, plus any freckles, moles, scars or facial hair.

Avoid: over-smoothed skin, a pasted-face or cutout look, no comic-panel lines, halftone dots or ink texture anywhere yet — this frame is entirely ordinary and photorealistic, the transformation has not started.
Промпт для видео Kling · Grok 
Create a short cinematic video based on the uploaded image. Vertical 9:16.
An ordinary bedroom. The person from the image stands facing camera; a Spider-Man movie poster hangs flat on the wall beside them.

Camera and motion structure:
One continuous slow pull-back, no cuts.
FRAMES 0–40%: Bold black comic-panel gutter lines crawl inward from the very edges of the frame, and halftone dot shading spreads across the walls and furniture, converting the room's surfaces into stylized ink-and-dot comic artwork while the person stays completely photorealistic against it.
FRAMES 40–70%: The Spider-Man figure peels itself off the poster in the same flat ink-and-halftone style and steps down into the room — still visibly a drawn, two-dimensional figure, never gaining real-world depth or texture — and raises a fist toward the person.
FRAMES 70–100%: The person meets the drawn fist with their own real fist. On contact, a single wordless white starburst flashes and fades at the point of impact — no letters, just a shape. The pull-back continues and settles wide, revealing the whole room now framed by a thick black comic-panel border at the very edges of the shot, the full scale of the transformed room visible at once as the ending.

Character:
Preserve the person's face, hair, skin tone and identity exactly from the source image throughout — they remain fully photorealistic at every moment, never gaining ink lines, panel borders or halftone texture on their own body or clothing. The drawn Spider-Man figure stays flat and two-dimensional throughout, never becoming photorealistic. Allowed motion: standing, the fist raise and fist-bump. Nothing else.

Style:
Graphic-novel ink and halftone environment against a photorealistic person — bold black gutters, dot-pattern shading, high contrast — the CRITICAL SEPARATION between the real person and the drawn world and drawn character must be maintained throughout.

Restrictions:
- Do not change the person's face, hair, skin tone or identity at any point, and never apply ink lines, panel borders or halftone dots to the person themselves
- The person stays in frame, recognizable and the visual focus for the entire video
- The person never speaks, sings or mouths words — silent physical action only
- No speech bubbles
- The fist-bump and its single wordless starburst flash happen exactly once — no letters, numbers or words appear anywhere in the starburst or anywhere else in the frame
- No additional gestures or beats beyond the ones described above
- No on-screen text, captions, titles or graphics of any kind anywhere in the video — the comic-panel border and halftone texture are visual style only, never lettering
- The drawn Spider-Man figure must never be rendered as a real, photorealistic person at any point
